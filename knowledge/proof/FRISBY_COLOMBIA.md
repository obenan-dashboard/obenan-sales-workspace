# Frisby Colombia: six-month Google results

Approved named public proof for Frisby's 213 locations in Colombia. Permission covers the
supplied aggregate figures and the metric-table screenshot, not logos, testimonials,
customer photos or underlying account records. See [the approval register](PUBLIC_PROOF_REGISTER.md).

## Ready-to-use pitch

Across Frisby's 213 locations in Colombia, Google profiles recorded 7.26 million impressions
and 534,616 customer actions from April to September 2026. Compared with the previous six
months, impressions rose 15% and actions rose 37%: 144,599 additional profile actions.
Call clicks increased 58%, with 103,203 additional call clicks, and website clicks more
than doubled, up 116%.

Percent changes are rounded to whole percentages, as in the supplied table. Interactions
reconcile to call clicks, direction requests, website clicks and menu clicks in each period.
Search and Maps impressions reconcile to total impressions.

## Strongest additional angles

- **More action, not just more exposure:** interactions grew faster than impressions,
  37% versus 15%. The aggregate action-to-impression ratio rose from about 6.2 to 7.4
  interactions per 100 impressions, a relative increase of about 19.4% using unrounded counts.
  This is an aggregate ratio, not a measured visitor-conversion rate.
- **More direct contact intent:** 281,163 call clicks, up from 177,960. Use "call clicks",
  not "103,203 extra phone calls".
- **Website engagement more than doubled:** 34,266 clicks, up from 15,880.
- **More navigation intent:** 215,511 direction requests, up from 193,916.

## Required limitations

- This compares consecutive six-month periods, **not a year-on-year comparison**.
  Seasonality may affect the comparison.
- Activation date and unchanged same-store coverage across the two periods are not
  established. Do not call the baseline "before Obenan" or assume the increase happened
  within the first six months after onboarding.
- Impressions are views, **not unique people**. Actions are events, not unique customers.
- Call clicks are **not completed calls**; direction requests are **not verified visits**.
  Clicks and interactions are not bookings, purchases or measured revenue.
- These are reported before/after results, **not causal attribution** to Obenan. No
  paid/organic split, attribution model or control group was supplied.
- No search-term breakdown was supplied. Do not infer nonbrand discovery from total
  impressions, and do not relabel Google results as measured AI-assistant visibility.
- This dated portfolio result is not a guarantee for another customer or today's performance.

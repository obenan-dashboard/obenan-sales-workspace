## What the whole interview supports

- **Two business needs, not just reviews:** deeper guest understanding across languages and
  cultures, and stronger brand visibility where guests are looking for somewhere to eat.
- **Three reasons for choosing Obenan:** technology already available, a collaborative process
  with frequent releases, and dedicated onboarding support.
- **Early pilot observations:** Joost reports more leads, qualified web visitors, higher
  rankings and visitors, with stronger uplift in tourist city-centre locations. No percentage,
  controlled causal comparison or monetary value is supplied in this interview.
- **Strategic value:** he wants Obenan to become a knowledge partner at the core of the
  company, connecting guest feedback with better service and loyalty. This is his ambition.
- **Efficiency:** he expects cross-country, multilingual guest understanding to be faster and
  more efficient. His “couple of full-time employees” comparison is conditional,
  not measured headcount savings.

At 4:48, expansion into 20 countries over six months is a stated plan, not a completed rollout.
The 200-plus/230-restaurant references describe the brand at recording, not the number managed
by Obenan or today's footprint. Do not infer either from this interview.

  proof page. Use one supporting quote only when it answers a specific concern.
- Outreach: one relevant excerpt at most, with attribution and a source link.
- LPQ's own results review: lead with LPQ's measured discovery results. The testimonial may
  support the narrative, but is not a substitute for current account evidence.
- Other customers' results reviews: do not insert LPQ proof by default or disrupt the
  eleven-page customer-first structure. Their own measured results remain the story.
- In a PDF, make the video link clickable and visible. Do not invent a customer photo,
  download a video still, or treat quotation approval as logo/image approval.
- Do not use this historical interview to substantiate current AI discovery, agent-led
  payments, exact destination counts, current synchronization cadence or guaranteed rankings.
- Pair separate approved metrics with their own dated source and scope. The interview does
  not validate those figures. Never convert views, actions or direction requests into guests
  or revenue without transaction evidence.

# Campos Coffee: two-café dated evidence card

**Approved named pitch use.** Campos Coffee's two cafés in Sydney and Melbourne.
Logo, quotes and photos are not cleared for use with other prospects.
Approval and review dates are in [the register](PUBLIC_PROOF_REGISTER.md).

Evidence captured 8 October 2026, from authorized profile performance, search-term and review
records. The source ledger and raw records remain in the private customer workspace.
Activation was 4 June 2026. Performance window: 4 June to 4 October 2026, compared with the
same dates in 2025. Reviews run to 8 October in both years. Calendar-month search data is a
different dataset; do not label all of June as post-activation.

## Approved measured facts and qualified findings

| Measure | Recorded figure | Scope or limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Google Search and Maps profile views | 184,027 | Two cafés, performance window. Views, not unique people. |
| Total profile actions | 16,774 vs 18,359 | Down 8.6% overall. Actions, not visits, transactions or unique guests. |
| Café A actions | 4,398 vs 2,969 | Up 48.1%, same performance window. |

| Korean, Chinese or Japanese terms | 12,716, 11.9% | Same 106,942 denominator; not visitor nationality or all-language demand. |
| New reviews | 83 vs 80 | Both periods 4 June to 8 October. Volume broadly flat, not a large growth story. |
| Reply coverage | 83 of 83 vs 80 of 80 | Already fully answered last year; no improvement in reply rate. |

| Reply voice | Default prompt configured | No evidence of a bespoke brand voice. |
| Review-request opens | 18 across two cafés | Operator-supplied dashboard count; not sent requests, unused requests, review conversions or a causal explanation of review volume. |

## Pitch wording

“Across Campos Coffee's two cafés in Sydney and Melbourne, about half the reviewed
search-term counts were non-brand queries. One café's profile actions rose from 2,969 to
4,398 over the matched four-month window, while the other declined. Both cafés' reviews
were fully answered. The operational opportunity is automating that work and fixing
verified profile gaps, not claiming Obenan caused every increase.”

## Do not reuse as a verified result

- A historical audit score as a current score.
- A 325-post schedule as 325 verified published posts.
- The previous 88-hour estimate as measured time saved or a defensible minimum. Delivery
  status and remaining staff effort were not fully established.
- An ad-equivalent range as revenue, ROI, paid-click equivalence or money actually saved.
  A proportional nonbrand action share is an inference, not measured attribution.
- A full destination roster as this customer's active directory connections.
- Review requests as the cause of reviews received or guaranteed ranking growth.

This is a dated, mixed-outcome example, not a promise of the same results for another business.

## Optional estimated scenario, not a measured result

The earlier deck's rounded A$4,800 to A$16,800 ad-cost equivalent is usable only with its
assumptions attached: 16,774 profile actions × (53,676 / 106,942) sampled nonbrand share gives about 8,419
inferred actions, priced at US$0.383 × assumed 1.50 AUD/USD, and an assumed A$2 ceiling.
CPC source: Australian estimates for 16 generic English queries, captured 8 October 2026.
Search-term and action windows/coverage differ. There is no measured attribution from queries
to actions, and profile actions are not paid clicks. Neither endpoint is spend saved or ROI.
No public FX source established the assumed rate. Do not reuse this as a current CPC quote.

# Urban Fork Group: year-on-year Google results

Approved named public proof for the supplied 12-location portfolio. Permission covers the
aggregate figures and supplied metric-table screenshot, not logos, testimonials, customer
photos or underlying account records. See [the approval register](PUBLIC_PROOF_REGISTER.md).

## Ready-to-use pitch

Across Urban Fork Group's 12 restaurants, Google-reported bookings rose 35% and Google
Maps impressions increased 44% from April to September 2026 compared with the same months
last year. Reported bookings reached 1,600, with 411 additional reported bookings. Total
Google profile impressions reached 1.02 million, up 42%, and direction requests rose 21%
to 32,566. Every listed customer-action category grew, including call clicks, website clicks
and menu clicks.

Bookings retain the supplied metric label; they are not fulfilled reservations or verified
guest arrivals. Direction requests are not verified visits. The reported interaction-total
comparison has an unresolved seven-action baseline discrepancy, detailed below.

## Reconciliation caveat

The 2025 action components sum to **49,574**, but the screenshot reports **49,581** total
interactions. This **seven-action** difference is **unresolved**. The 2026 components sum
exactly to 57,943. Preserve the reported totals; do not silently correct them or claim the
entire table fully reconciles. Request source clarification before removing this caveat.
The reported +17% and 8,362-action increase use 49,581 as the baseline, not the component sum.

## Strongest additional angles

- **More reported bookings:** 1,600 versus 1,189, up 35%, with 411 additional reported bookings.
  The screenshot calls these "Bookings"; the source-system definition is unverified.
- **More Maps visibility:** 756,460 impressions versus 524,086, up 44%. No query breakdown
  was supplied, so this does not establish nearby-intent or nonbrand searches.
- **More navigation intent:** 32,566 direction requests versus 27,006, with 5,560 additional
  direction requests. This is not measured footfall.
- **Broader engagement growth:** call clicks +20%, website clicks +10%, menu clicks +8%.

## Required limitations

- This is a same-months year-on-year comparison. Activation date and stable same-store
  coverage across the two periods are unverified. Do not call 2025 "before Obenan".
- Impressions are views, **not unique people**. Actions are events, not unique customers.
- Call clicks are **not completed calls**; direction requests are **not verified visits**.
  Reported bookings are **not fulfilled reservations**, purchases or measured revenue.
- Interactions per 100 impressions fell from approximately **6.89** to **5.66**, a **17.9% decline**
  using reported totals. This is an aggregate action-to-impression ratio, **not a visitor-conversion rate**.
  Do not claim improved conversion efficiency; impressions grew faster than interactions.
- This is reported comparative performance, **not causal attribution** to Obenan. No
  control group, paid/organic split or attribution model was supplied.
- Google metrics do not establish AI-assistant visibility or nonbrand discovery.
- These dated results are not today's performance or a guarantee for another customer.

## Reuse

Lead with reported bookings and Maps visibility, keeping the customer name, Google scope,
period and metric definitions nearby. If quoting total actions or their increase, carry the
baseline discrepancy qualification. Use this card as the single source of figures; do not
copy them into other playbooks or substitute them for another customer's own results.

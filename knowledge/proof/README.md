# Reusable proof

[PUBLIC_PROOF_REGISTER.md](PUBLIC_PROOF_REGISTER.md) is the approval authority.
Each story is stored once. Link to its evidence card; do not copy figures into other playbooks.

Name, metrics, quote and image permissions are separate. Approved anonymous metrics do not
grant naming, photo or testimonial permission. Anonymisation alone is not permission to
publish: exact aggregates may still identify the business.

Agents may pitch approved aggregates with the recorded period, geography and limitations.
Do not turn a historical proof card into current performance, transfer its figures to another
customer, or combine a name from another source with a name-pending anonymous card.

Raw exports, account identifiers, emails, invoices, contracts and customer photos stay private.
Publication of a curated aggregate card is an explicit exception, not permission to publish
underlying records. Review or withdraw approval when the register's review date is reached.

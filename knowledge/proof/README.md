# Reusable proof

[PUBLIC_PROOF_REGISTER.md](PUBLIC_PROOF_REGISTER.md) is the approval authority.
Each story is stored once. Link to its evidence card; do not copy figures into other playbooks.

For country-specific pitches, [the country account index](CUSTOMER_COUNTRIES.md) supplies one
canonical names-and-countries extract. It records location markets, not headquarters,
whole-chain coverage or paid-customer status. Logo and performance permissions remain separate.

Use approved customer names, not anonymized descriptors, under Seven's 9 October 2026
named-story approval. [LPQ's results and testimonial card](LE_PAIN_QUOTIDIEN.md) contains
the three-year measured results, matched comparisons, estimate boundaries, source provenance,
full supplied transcript, strongest exact excerpts and PDF placement rules. Campos Coffee's existing
[two-café card](SPECIALTY_COFFEE_TWO_CAFES.md) retains its filename and dated figures.
[Frisby's Colombia card](FRISBY_COLOMBIA.md) supplies six-month Google results across 213
locations, with the approved source screenshot, reconciled totals and consecutive-period limits.
[Urban Fork Group's card](URBAN_FORK_GROUP.md) supplies same-months year-on-year Google
results across 12 restaurants, led by reported bookings and Maps visibility. Its seven-action
baseline discrepancy and metric-definition limits remain explicit.

The [customer logo index](../../assets/customer-logos/README.md) supplies nine approved Demo
marks with provenance. Customer information Seven explicitly stores for public sharing is
usable within its recorded scope; customer origin alone is not a privacy blocker.

Name, metrics, quote and image permissions are separate. Approved anonymous metrics do not
grant naming, photo or testimonial permission. Anonymisation alone is not permission to
publish: exact aggregates may still identify the business.

Agents may pitch approved aggregates with the recorded period, geography and limitations.
Do not turn a historical proof card into current performance, transfer its figures to another
customer, or combine a name from another source with a name-pending anonymous card.

Raw exports, account identifiers, emails, invoices, contracts and customer photos stay private.
Publication of a curated aggregate card is an explicit exception, not permission to publish
underlying records. Review or withdraw approval when the register's review date is reached.

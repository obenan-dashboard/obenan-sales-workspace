# Public proof register

This is the authority for reusable customer names, testimonials, metrics and partner claims in
this public repository. An agent may use only items marked `APPROVED` and only within the
exact scope shown here.

Public naming permission does not authorize internal account identifiers, location lists,
user or role counts, private correspondence, contracts, billing information or unpublished
performance data.

## Named-story policy

Seven approved publishing named customer success stories on 9 October 2026. Do not anonymize
an approved named story by default. Record the exact name, sourced claims and approval in this
register for every new story. This approval does not make an unsupported claim true, publish
private source records, or license every logo, photo, quotation or partner reference.

## Adding a customer success story

Add one section per customer with all fields below. Record separate permission statuses.
Do not merge a story while any required field is missing.

```text
### Customer name

Status: APPROVED | PENDING | EXPIRED
Name status:
Metrics status:
Quote and image status:
Approved use:
Public source URL:
Approved claims and metrics:
Measurement window:
Geography and scope:
Material limitations:
Prohibited details:
Approved by:
Approval recorded:
Review date:
```

Rules:

- Each item used must be approved: a name needs naming approval, each metric needs metrics
  approval. Metrics may be approved anonymously while naming remains pending.
- Link to a public source whenever one exists.
- State the measurement window, geography and relevant limitations.
- Never infer permission from an old deck, email, contract or private dashboard.
- Expired and pending items are not usable in customer-facing material.

# Agentic closing pair

Every Obenan deck ends on two locked pages: **What comes next?** and **See it in three
minutes**. Seven locked them on 9 October 2026 after the first results review that used them.
The copy lives in the two deck templates; this note says where the pair goes and what may move.

## Placement

- Results review: pages 10 and 11, the last two pages.
- Prospect deck: pages 8 and 9, followed only by the decision page.

## What may change

- Results review, page 10: the one agreed next step.
- Film page: the closing line, and only to state a verified, approved agentic build for this
  customer. Everything else, headlines, the two-column role split and the payment line, is fixed.
- Translate natively; keep the film's title in its original wording.

## The film

- The film is Visa Intelligent Commerce, public on YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9WCTYK-iHb0
- Link the thumbnail and print the URL, so the page works on screen and on paper. The
  thumbnail is loaded from YouTube at render time and is never stored in this repository.
- Open the rendered PDF and confirm the thumbnail shows and the link is clickable.

## Boundaries

- Agent-led transactions are pilot-stage. Obenan does not process payments.
- No timeline, security review status, named pilot merchant or share-of-work figure.
- No other partner is named. Approval scope is in the register's partner section.

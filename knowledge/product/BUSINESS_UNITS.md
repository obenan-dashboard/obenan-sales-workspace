# Obenan capability tracks

Use this distinction to keep a sales conversation inside the right scope. The tracks share a
foundation of approved merchant facts, but they solve different problems.

## 1. Agentic Location Management

**One line:** Obenan keeps a business's local facts, activity and reputation accurate and
current across supported public destinations.

Customer problems:

- facts drift across maps, directories and search surfaces
- local content and reviews create recurring work
- central teams cannot inspect every location consistently
- portfolio-wide changes still require local detail and governance

Relevant capabilities:

- listing and business-information management
- supported profile creation and correction
- local content coordination
- review and question workflows
- reporting and portfolio insight
- AI visibility inspection and verification
- governed access through compatible assistants

Lead with customer outcomes: accurate information, consistent execution, lower operational
load and a stronger public fact base. Confirm the contracted destinations, fields, cadence and
approval model.

## 2. Merchant Infrastructure and Agent Layer

**One line:** Obenan makes approved merchant facts and permitted capabilities usable by
compatible assistants and agent workflows under governance.

Customer or partner problems:

- assistants cannot reliably identify which merchant facts to trust
- permission and confirmation are required before an agent can act
- discovery, merchant truth and downstream action need clear boundaries

Relevant capabilities:

- machine-readable merchant facts
- governed portfolio queries
- authenticated, permissioned requests
- approval and confirmation steps
- compatibility with supported agent clients and workflows

Lead with governed access and reliable merchant truth. Do not imply unrestricted self-service,
general availability of a private pilot, a guaranteed AI recommendation or payment processing.

## How the tracks connect

Agentic Location Management improves the public record and recurring location operation. The
Agent Layer makes approved facts and permitted actions available to compatible assistants.
Together they support a journey from discovery to a governed next action, but each commercial
promise must match the customer's actual scope.

## Routing questions

1. Is the buyer trying to manage physical locations and reputation? Start with track 1.
2. Is the buyer integrating assistant workflows or governed agent access? Start with track 2.
3. Does the opportunity use both? Explain them separately, then show the connection.
4. Does the request involve a partner, transaction or private pilot claim? Stop unless the
   exact public wording is approved in the proof register.

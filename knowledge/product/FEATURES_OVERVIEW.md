## Capability families

### Business information and listings

- Govern core facts such as name, address, phone, website, categories and location status.
- Keep regular and special hours, services, attributes and supported catalog information
  aligned across connected destinations.
- Create or correct supported profiles and suppress duplicate listings where the delivery scope
  allows it.
- Coordinate portfolio-level updates while preserving location-specific facts.

### Content and local activity

- Prepare and publish approved local updates to supported channels.
- Manage approved photos and media where destination support exists.
- Keep the workflow moving without adding recurring manual work to the customer's team.

Do not promise a daily cadence or a specific social channel unless it appears in the agreed
scope.

### Reputation

- Monitor reviews and recurring customer themes.
- Draft replies in the customer's approved tone.
- Publish replies after approval, or under a configured auto-reply rule where contracted.
- Answer supported customer questions from approved business facts.

Never promise review gating, removal of valid negative reviews or universal reply coverage.

### Reporting and portfolio insight

- Bring location, visibility and reputation signals into a portfolio view.
- Support governed reporting for authorized users.
- Help teams identify exceptions, patterns and locations that need attention.

Confirm enabled report types, cadence, branding and delivery method for each customer.

### AI visibility

- Inspect what supported AI assistants currently say about a business.
- Identify facts that are missing, stale, conflicting or hard to verify.
- Correct approved facts across public sources and machine-readable surfaces within scope.
- Recheck observed answers and source evidence over time.
- Offer a public AI Presence Audit for the current public picture of a business.

Do not promise ranking, citation, recommendation or a date by which an external AI answer will
change.

### Agent Layer

- Make governed Obenan capabilities available to compatible assistants and agent workflows.
- Let authorized users query connected data and request permitted actions.
- Keep authentication, permissions, confirmation and approval rules in control.

Access is onboarding-led. Never imply unrestricted public self-service or permission to act on
an account the user does not control.

## Not authorized as a default claim

- Every field is supported on every destination.
- Obenan controls or guarantees an AI assistant's answer.
- All review replies publish without customer governance.
- Every customer receives the same integrations, automation, reporting or white-label scope.
- Obenan processes payments.
- A private pilot, roadmap item or internal test is generally available.

## How to use this file

1. Match the prospect's problem to one or two capability families.
2. Verify exact destination and account scope when it changes the commercial promise.
3. Use the live public destination roster for exact coverage counts.
4. Use the public proof register before naming another customer or quoting customer results.
5. When a requested capability is not documented here or on a current public source, say it
   needs confirmation rather than inventing an answer.

# Language and claim boundaries

Last updated: 2026-07-08  
Owner: Obenan
Audience: Sales, agencies, resellers, content and design

## Purpose

Use this guardrail before writing website copy, ad copy, landing pages, sales decks,
outreach, workshop outputs or customer stories.

The goal is not to restrict creativity. The goal is to stop Obenan from being pulled into old categories that make the product sound like a dashboard, a tool, a local SEO vendor, or a review-management add-on.

## Source Basis

This document is based on approved positioning and the public-safe product canon in this
repository. Private transcripts and account data are not copied here.

## Core Rule

Obenan should be positioned as the autonomous AI-native layer that keeps every location found, trusted, and chosen across the modern discovery ecosystem.

Do not position Obenan as something the customer logs into and operates. Position it as something that operates for them.

## Forbidden Terms

| Do not use | Why it is banned | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Local SEO | Anchors Obenan to an old, crowded service category and hides the AI discovery shift. | AI-native digital presence, agentic location management, discovery layer, AI-readable digital presence |
| SEO tool | Makes Obenan sound narrow, tactical, and commodity. | Discovery infrastructure, machine-readable digital presence, technical discovery foundation |
| Location marketing software | Sounds like a legacy MarTech system. | Agentic location management, autonomous digital presence management |
| Review management tool | Reduces Obenan to one feature. | Intelligent review management as one capability inside a broader AI workforce |
| Reputation booster | Booster sounds optional and temporary. | Found, trusted, and chosen; trust intelligence; reputation building |
| Online visibility booster | Too 2021, too narrow, too close to legacy local SEO. | AI-era digital presence intelligence |
| Listing management tool | Reduces the product to directory sync. | Digital presence orchestration, cross-system synchronization |
| Platform, as the main label | Implies another system a human must learn and operate. | AI workforce, autonomous intelligence, agentic location management |
| Tool, as the main label | Makes Obenan sound small and manually operated. | AI workforce, autonomous intelligence, capability |
| Software, as the main label | Sounds like another login and workload. | AI-native system, autonomous intelligence, AI workforce |
| Dashboard, as the main value proposition | Suggests reporting without execution. | Intelligence that acts, prescriptive intelligence, automated intelligence delivery |
| AI-powered | Sounds like legacy software with AI added later. | AI-native, agentic, autonomous |
| AI add-on | Minimizes architecture and makes AI sound optional. | AI-native architecture, autonomous AI workforce |
| Automated platform | Still sounds like a platform. | Autonomous AI workforce |
| Log in to manage | Reinforces manual operation. | Your AI manages it for you |
| Features | Too product-led and internal. | Capabilities your AI handles for you |
| Chores | Makes the customer pain sound small. | The digital side of running a business |
| Hassle | Understates the business risk. | Operational load, digital complexity, technology gap |
| Cheap | Destroys perceived value and attracts price-only buyers. | Accessible, democratized, built for scale |
| Affordable | Still anchors the conversation to price. | Accessible, efficient, commercially practical |
| Price, cost, fee in brand copy | Puts spend before value. | Investment, commercial model, monthly commitment |
| Netflix subscription | Creates low-commitment consumer pricing psychology. | No replacement in positioning. Keep price out of brand copy |
| Unlike [named competitor] | Puts Obenan inside their category and invites comparison. | Legacy approaches wait for humans to act. Obenan sees, decides, and acts |
| Better than competitors | Makes the market frame about existing vendors. | Built for the discovery era, not the old dashboard era |
| All-in-one platform | Generic SaaS language with no category leadership. | One AI workforce for every location |
| Learn more | Passive CTA. | Get Your Free AI Audit, See What Your AI Finds, Book a Demo |
| Explore | Passive CTA. | See What Your AI Finds, Get Your Location Audit |

## Use With Care

These terms are not fully banned, but they must stay in the right context.

| Term | Allowed context | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| SEO | Technical implementation, content strategy, machine readability, search infrastructure. | Headlines, category labels, landing-page hero copy, sales deck positioning. |
| Dashboard | Reporting, executive visibility and account health. | Main product promise or first-frame positioning. |
| Platform | Technical conversations, integration architecture, procurement descriptions. | Brand category, ads, homepage hero, opening sales narrative. |
| AI visibility | ObiGEO, audit/reporting, specific outcome proof. | Replacing the whole Obenan category with a narrow visibility claim. |
| Agentic | Category and strategic language for sophisticated buyers. | Repeating it without plain-language explanation for restaurant operators. |
| MCP | Technical partner, agentic operations, developer or enterprise architecture. | Restaurant owner copy, hospitality landing-page hero, paid ad headline. |
| Reviews | Outcome proof and capability descriptions. | Defining Obenan as a review company. |
| Google Maps | Concrete channel proof and customer examples. | Defining Obenan as a Google Maps optimizer only. |
| Marketing | GTM conversation and buyer context. | Making Obenan sound like a marketing agency. |

## Q3 Pillar Language

Unmuted is currently using three active pillars and one future pillar.

| Pillar | Customer-facing meaning | Copy rule |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | Every location becomes easier for guests and AI assistants to find, verify, and recommend. | Do not reduce this to SEO. |
| Automate | Repetitive location work is handled continuously across channels. | Do not reduce this to scheduling posts. |
| Act | The system does not only report. It changes, publishes, responds, synchronizes, and escalates. | Strengthen this in decks and pages. |
| Trust | Future pillar. Proof, credibility, reviews, and data quality that make locations recommendable. | Use as strategic direction, not as a launched product promise unless confirmed. |

## StoryBrand Rules

Every customer-facing draft should pass these rules:

- The customer is the hero. Obenan is the guide.
- Lead with the customer pain or desired outcome, not product architecture.
- Make the threat concrete but brief: if AI cannot find you, customers cannot find you.
- Paint success more than failure.
- Translate every capability into an outcome a restaurant or hospitality leader can feel.
- Avoid competitor comparison. Fight the technology gap, not other vendors.
- Use the founder story as empathy and authority, not as autobiography.

## Channel Rules

| Channel | Rule |
|---|---|
| Homepage and hospitality landing pages | No local SEO, no tool framing, no dashboard-led hero. Open with discovery, guest action, and the technology gap. |
| Ads | Keep language plain. One pain, one outcome, one action. Do not explain architecture in the headline. |
| Sales deck | Use the pillars Discover, Automate, Act. Make ACT visibly about execution, not reporting. |
| LinkedIn founder posts | Repeat the hospitality and discovery narrative. Use restaurant-owner empathy and founder-market fit. |
| Case studies | Lead with business outcomes and customer language. Use feature detail only after the result is clear. |
| Technical content | SEO, JSON, LLM TXT, structured data, caching, and machine-readable content are allowed, but should support the discovery narrative. |
| Workshops | Test customer language. Do not copy competitor language. Do not let early draft terms become official without review. |

## Review Checklist For Unmuted

Before any draft leaves Unmuted:

- Check for forbidden words in the table above.
- If SEO, platform, dashboard, or tool appears, confirm it is a technical or internal context.
- Confirm Obenan sounds like it operates for the customer, not something the customer operates.
- Confirm the copy says what changes for the buyer.
- Confirm the copy can be understood by a nontechnical restaurant or hospitality executive.
- Confirm the call to action is active.
- Confirm there is no competitor comparison.
- Confirm the draft supports the Q3 focus: multi-location hospitality, Benelux paid focus, DACH and UK organic support.

Obenan keeps every location found, trusted, and chosen across Google, Apple Maps, directories, and AI assistants, with an AI-native system that acts continuously on the business's behalf.

# Obenan value proposition

## Core promise

Obenan helps businesses with physical locations stay found, trusted and chosen wherever
customers look: maps, directories, reviews, search and AI assistants. It keeps approved
business facts accurate, prepares and coordinates local activity, and turns reputation and
visibility signals into action.

The customer defines the facts, standards, permissions and approval policy. Obenan handles the
recurring work within that governed scope.

## The customer problem

Multi-location businesses face four connected problems:

1. Important business facts drift across public destinations.
2. Reviews, questions, photos and local updates create recurring operational work.
3. Central teams cannot see and act on every location consistently.
4. AI assistants assemble answers from public sources that may be stale or conflicting.

The result is not only a reporting problem. Customers may see the wrong hours, incomplete
services, inconsistent locations or an uncertain picture of the brand before they visit or
book.

## What changes with Obenan

### One governed business record

Approved facts are maintained centrally and distributed to supported destinations. Coverage
varies by field and destination, so every proposal should state the relevant scope.

### Recurring work moves off the customer's desk

Obenan prepares or performs permitted listing, content, reputation and reporting work under the
customer's approval policy. The value is consistent execution, not another dashboard to watch.

### Portfolio exceptions become visible

Teams can identify recurring themes, missing information and locations that need attention
without manually checking every surface.

### AI visibility becomes governable

Obenan inspects how supported assistants represent the business, improves the approved public
facts they retrieve from, and rechecks observed answers over time. External AI answers remain
outside Obenan's control.

### Compatible assistants can become an operating surface

Through the Agent Layer, authorized users can query connected data and request permitted
actions from compatible assistants. Authentication, permissions, confirmation and approvals
still apply.

## Business outcomes to lead with

- More accurate and consistent customer-facing information.
- Less recurring manual work for central and local teams.
- Faster portfolio-wide updates with local detail preserved.
- More consistent review and question handling.
- Clearer evidence of where visibility or trust is breaking down.
- A more reliable public fact base for search and AI discovery.

Do not turn these into guaranteed ranking, recommendation, footfall, booking or revenue claims.
Those outcomes require customer evidence and a stated measurement method.

## Best-fit customers

- Restaurant groups, hotels, retail networks and franchises.
- Businesses whose customers depend on accurate local information.
- Central teams responsible for many locations.
- Agencies or resellers operating location portfolios under an agreed commercial and access
  model.

Pure e-commerce businesses without a physical location are not the primary fit.

## Positioning by buyer

### Marketing leader

Lead with discovery, brand consistency, reputation insight and measurable customer actions.

### Operations leader

Lead with accurate facts, exception handling, governance and reduced recurring work.

### Digital or product leader

Lead with structured public facts, supported destinations, governed integrations and the Agent
Layer. Avoid unsupported implementation detail.

### Agency or reseller

Lead with portfolio operation, repeatable reporting and a governed customer model. Confirm
white-label, access and support scope in the commercial agreement.

## Claim boundaries

- Say `104 integrated directories and platforms, 100 currently updating` only when using the
  live public roster reviewed on the date of the deliverable. Otherwise say `more than 100`.
- Say capability varies by destination and field.
- Describe review replies as approval-based or governed by a configured rule.
- Describe AI visibility as inspection, correction and verification, not control of a model.
- Describe the Agent Layer as onboarding-led and permissioned.
- Do not name a partner, pilot or customer unless the public proof register permits the exact
  use.
- Do not describe private pilots, roadmap items or internal tests as generally available.
- Do not say Obenan processes payments.

## Evidence hierarchy for public sales work

1. Current public Obenan product pages and destination roster.
2. Current approved public proof register.
3. Customer-specific data retrieved with authorized access for that customer's deliverable.
4. Contracted scope confirmed by an Obenan owner.

When sources conflict or a current promise depends on private release status, stop and ask the
Obenan owner. Never copy internal specifications into this public repository.

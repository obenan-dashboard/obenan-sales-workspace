# What’s new in Obenan

Checked against the English production site on 24 November 2026. Use the product pages to explain what a merchant can do and the monthly release notes to check when a feature became available. Verify the customer's setup before quoting account-specific results.

## Which product answers the buyer’s question?

| Buyer’s question | What sales can show |
|---|---|
| “Can AI read what we sell at this location?” | **ObiGEO** publishes location facts and offerings on the merchant’s own domain in a form people and AI assistants can read. Start here for menus, services or opening hours. The pages include structured data. Publication alone does not establish that an assistant recommended the merchant. |
| “Can we keep our website?” | **ObiProxy** serves the selected facts and location pages on the merchant’s connected domain. The existing site continues to serve the homepage, menu and shop. Check the domain setup with the merchant. |
| “Which services read our pages?” | **ObiAnalytics** shows recorded page requests by tracked AI and search services over time. Participating merchants see **Agent traffic** once their website logs are connected. The public example uses sample counts. Repeat requests count separately. A gap in recording means the traffic is unknown. A request does not establish a shopper’s query, a citation, a visit or a sale. |
| “How can a shopping assistant find our locations and offerings?” | **Merchant Discovery** gives compatible assistants current merchant and product information. In the published pilot an agent found a merchant, built a cart and reached the checkout handoff. The merchant’s checkout and payment provider handle payment. Confirm access and pilot scope for the particular opportunity. |
| “How do we keep our locations current?” | **Agentic Location Management** helps teams maintain business facts across supported destinations. Start with the locations and channels the customer actually manages. |

## Product and interface changes

**November.** The Reviews workspace went live on 10 November. On 16 November the reporting route switched to the new reports. The release also documents a merchant publishing facts on its main website and seeing recorded site requests. Check availability for the account you are showing.

https://www.obenan.ai/product-updates/2026-11/

**October.** Teams could compare groups of locations in Google reporting. The Google report became generally available on 20 October. The ObiGEO dashboard shown that month was a preview with sample data.

https://www.obenan.ai/product-updates/2026-10/

**September.** Verified Facts gave teams a way to work with checked merchant information. Enabled accounts could compare Google reporting year over year. The team reviewed WhatsApp consent that month.

https://www.obenan.ai/product-updates/2026-09/

**August.** Source Map showed which sources ChatGPT and Gemini cited. Fix Queue gave teams a list of issues to act on. A recorded Merchant Discovery demo followed a guest cart to checkout handoff.

https://www.obenan.ai/product-updates/2026-08/

**July.** White-label setup and multi-location onboarding supported branded rollouts. The update also covered Maps Ranker for tracking local rankings.

https://www.obenan.ai/product-updates/2026-07/

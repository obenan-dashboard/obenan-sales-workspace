# Sales Psychology

Prospects do not buy software. They buy confidence that their business will be found, trusted, and chosen consistently.

## Four Buying Drivers

### 1) Discoverability Growth
Buyer question: Will more of the right customers find us?
Signals: weak non-branded visibility; reliance on paid ads; low map or search presence.

### 2) Operational Relief
Buyer question: Will this remove manual digital workload?
Signals: repetitive profile tasks; review response backlog; inconsistent updates.

### 3) Revenue and Conversion Lift
Buyer question: Will this lead to more customer actions and sales?
Signals: profile traffic without action growth; low call, direction, or booking conversion.

### 4) Risk and Control
Buyer question: Can we trust this across all locations and over time?
Signals: data inconsistency; location-level quality variance; no reliable reporting rhythm.

## Hidden Buyer Fears
- this will be another tool we must manage daily
- we have heard this from agencies before
- we will spend money and see no real outcome
- changing process will create more chaos

Counter-pattern: stay practical, use their data, recommend a simple first step.

## Decision Acceleration Rules
1. specificity beats persuasion
2. clarity beats complexity
3. proof beats promise
4. direction beats options overload

## Stakes and Success Balance
Use stakes lightly: if AI cannot find you, customers cannot find you.
Then paint success: accurate listings, fast review responses, active digital presence, less manual effort, more customer actions.

Rule: one sentence of stakes, three sentences of success.

## Authority Without Pressure
Show authority through calm diagnosis, smart questions, clear recommendations.
Avoid hype, superlatives, competitor comparisons, hard pressure closes.

# Operational Diagnostic Framework

Source: Wolfgang Sales Agent V2, 14_OperationalDiagnosticFramework.md. Extracted 2026-10-05.

Identify where visibility, trust, and conversion are leaking, then prescribe the next high-impact fix.

## Diagnostic Model

### 1) Demand Capture
How do customers usually search for you today?
Check branded versus non-branded discoverability, search and maps dependency, AI discovery relevance.

### 2) Listing Integrity
Are your business details consistent across Google, Apple Maps, Bing, and key directories?
Check NAP consistency, category fit, profile completeness, update reliability across locations.

### 3) Reputation Engine
How are reviews generated today? How fast are reviews answered?
Check review velocity, response coverage, response quality.

### 4) Activity Engine
How often is your profile actively updated? Who owns ongoing content and posts?
Check posting cadence, freshness signals, operational discipline.

### 5) Conversion Readiness
When users find you, does the profile convert into calls, directions, or bookings?
Check visual quality, offer clarity, CTA clarity, friction in the booking or contact path.

### 6) Scale and Control
How many locations are involved? How much is still handled manually?
Check multi-location governance, reporting maturity, manual workload burden.

## High-Priority Signals
- inconsistent location data
- weak or irregular review flow
- delayed or missing review responses
- no repeatable profile activity rhythm
- disconnected location management process
- high manual effort for low strategic value

## Diagnosis Output Pattern
Das heisst, der Hauptengpass liegt bei [layer]. That is currently reducing [visibility, trust, or customer actions]. First move should be [specific action], then [second action].

## When to Suggest a Call or Demo
At least two high-priority signals present, the decision owner engaged, and the prospect acknowledges business impact.

Note: this framework maps one to one onto the MCP evidence runbook. Each layer above has a
measurable counterpart. Diagnose with data rather than with questions alone wherever the
account is already on Obenan.

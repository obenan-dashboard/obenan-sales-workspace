# Conversation Patterns

Source: Wolfgang Sales Agent V2, 13_ConversationPatterns.md. Extracted 2026-10-05.
Templates below carry Wolfgang's German and English mix as written. Adapt per market.

## Pattern 1: Precision Outreach
1. personalized context 2. observed visibility gap 3. outcome-based value hypothesis 4. direct CTA

> Hi [Name], great connecting with you at [context]. What stood out to me is [specific gap]. We help businesses close this gap so they are found, trusted, and chosen more often, without adding manual workload. Get Your Free AI Audit this week and we can map the exact opportunity together.

## Pattern 2: Discovery Opener
> Super, danke dir for taking the time. If it is okay, I would like to keep this practical and understand how customers currently find you online. Wie sucht eure Zielgruppe heute nach euch?

## Pattern 3: Diagnostic Bridge
> Genau, das heisst ihr habt [issue]. That usually impacts visibility and customer actions directly. Wie viel Zeit verliert euer Team aktuell pro Woche dadurch?

## Pattern 4: Value Prescription
> The lever here is simple: consistency, review speed, and profile activity. When this runs continuously, discoverability usually moves quickly. If useful, we can map your setup and show exactly what should be automated first.

## Pattern 5: Post-Call Recap
> Thanks again for today. What we aligned on: [3 bullets]. Next steps: [owner, action, deadline]. I will send the [audit or proposal] by [date], and we reconnect on [date or time].

## Pattern 6: Silence Recovery
> Hi [Name], quick follow-up from my side. We identified one clear opportunity around [issue] that could improve discoverability without extra team effort. If relevant, we can review it in 15 minutes.

## Pattern 7: Soft Close
> Understood, thanks for the clarity. If this becomes a priority later, the key lever remains the same: if AI cannot find you, customers cannot find you. Happy to revisit when timing is right.

# Sales Objection Playbook

Use this structure:
1. acknowledge
2. clarify
3. reframe with business impact
4. confirm the next step

Never argue. Never become defensive.

## We already use another tool or agency
Makes complete sense. Many teams already have a setup but still see gaps in consistency or response speed. Which part is currently working well, and where do you still see manual effort? If useful, we can run a quick audit and map only the gap.

## We can do this ourselves
Absolutely, and many businesses start that way. The challenge is usually consistency over time, especially across locations and directories. How much weekly time is your team currently spending on this? If it is high, we should look at what can be automated first.

## We do not have time
Understood. That is exactly the core issue we solve. The digital side still has to run, even when nobody has time for it. Which part currently eats the most time: reviews, updates, or content?

## Too expensive
Fair point. Usually the better question is not cost, but what manual work and missed visibility currently cost you every month. If the investment does not outperform that, it is not worth it. Let us quantify it together before deciding.

## Why recurring subscription
Good question. Discoverability is ongoing, not one-time. Data changes, reviews come in, and platforms keep moving. If activity stops, visibility drops over time.

## Send me information
Happy to. To send something useful, I need two or three details about your current setup first. Otherwise it will be generic and not helpful. Can I ask you three quick questions now?

## Maybe later
Totally fair. Is this a timing issue or a priority issue right now? If timing, we can lock a light checkpoint and revisit with your data.

## Not interested
Understood, thanks for the direct answer. Before I close this, one quick question: is your digital side already fully handled, or just not a focus right now? Either way, happy to leave it here.

## We do not need review management
I hear you. The key is not only sentiment. It is response behavior and trust signals for search and AI discovery. How consistently are reviews currently answered across all locations?

## Who are you exactly
Good question. I lead sales at Obenan and we help businesses stay found, trusted, and chosen online without adding manual workload. I reached out because I saw potential in your current setup and thought a short audit could be useful.

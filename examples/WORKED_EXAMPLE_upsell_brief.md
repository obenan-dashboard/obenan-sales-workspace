### 1. The hook: 720,000 nonbrand searches surfaced your locations

### 2. Put a price on that demand

Value the discovery volume as paid media. This is the number that makes it a no-brainer

Do it honestly:
- Source a real cost-per-click for the restaurant and dining category in [country]. Cite

### 3. Reframe the falling clicks. This is the pivot to ObiGEO.

Impressions held better than clicks. Calls, website clicks and menu views fell while
BOOKINGS ROSE 20 percent. The decision is still happening. The
click is not.

The mechanism to explain: the answer is now assembled before the visit. Increasingly the
thing reading a restaurant's public record is a machine preparing an answer, not a person
browsing a site. When the answer is complete, the human never clicks, and they book.

Be disciplined here. You cannot prove from Google data that agents caused the decline,
and you must not say you can. State the mechanism, show that bookings rose while clicks
fell, and then make the testable offer: give us your website analytics and we will show
you, on your own domain, how much of your traffic is already machines.

That is the bridge to ObiGEO. If the machine is the new reader, the record it reads has
to be managed, and that is what activating the full portfolio unlocks.

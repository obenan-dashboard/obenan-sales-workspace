The governance layer

One approved record per branch and per ATM, with a named owner.

What the bank would hold in one place before anything is published. A proposal to agree, not a description of the bank's current setup.

  • Branches and ATMs apart

    Each has its own listing, hours and owner, even when an ATM sits inside a branch.

  • One bank record

    The bank's own record is the source each listing is compared with.

  • A named approver per field

    Hours, services, closures and public wording each have an owner who signs off.

  • Unknown stays Unknown

    A listing that has not been checked is shown as Unknown, never as zero or as fine.

  • Proposed, then approved

    Every change is shown as a proposal first. Nothing is sent automatically.

  • Where customers look

    The roster lists 104 destinations, 4 marked inactive. Which ones apply depends on the country and the account.

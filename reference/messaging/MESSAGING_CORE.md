# Messaging Core v2

Status: PROPOSED / NOT APPROVED

Research cutoff: 2026-08-19

This is the first messaging file future website, sales-deck, communication-design, and product-copy agents should read. It governs proposals only. It is not approved copy, product proof, or release authority.

## Messaging north star

Acknowledge that AI keeps changing, then make Obenan feel like the capable colleague who carries the supported ongoing work while the customer stays informed and in control.

## The customer's human tension

The external condition is enough: AI keeps changing. A prospect may care about what that means for their business. Do not invent the rest of their biography.

Never declare or imply that the prospect is behind, overwhelmed, under-skilled, time-poor, hiring, short-staffed, already using a particular system, or following a particular workflow. Recognition should invite identification, not diagnose it.

## Bounded relief promise

The intended relief is concrete: the customer should not have to personally follow every relevant change or carry every supported recurring task. Obenan may be described as carrying work only when the exact work, cadence, channel, authorization, customer control, evidence, exceptions, and boundary are current and proven.

Relief is not a promise that everything is automatic, complete, permanent, or outside the customer's responsibility. The customer remains informed and retains judgment where the product or risk requires it.

## Story roles

- Customer: the hero, pursuing their own business outcome.
- Obenan: the guide, showing warmth through recognition and competence through evidence.
- AI change: an external condition, not a villain and not proof of customer failure.
- Product mechanism: supporting evidence, not the protagonist.
- Next step: a clear invitation that preserves the customer's freedom to decide.

StoryBrand is a drafting framework here, not causal science. The relationship is useful. Its fixed formulas, ratios, and conversion claims are not adopted.

## Message sequence

1. Recognition: name the external change or a directly evidenced customer concern.
2. Relief: state the practical burden Obenan can credibly carry.
3. Credible mechanism or proof: show supported scope, evidence, or a verified example.
4. Retained control: make approval, correction, visibility, limits, and exceptions clear where material.
5. Next step: offer one truthful, low-pressure action.

The sequence is a working editorial pattern, not a behavioral law. Use only the moves the surface needs, but never let brevity erase proof or control.

## Voice

### Attributes

Warm, peer-to-peer, capable, concise, direct, calm, concrete, respectful, platform-neutral, honest about limits, and adult-to-adult.

### Anti-attributes

Instructional, professorial, technical-first, sentimental, magical, fear-led, shaming, status-coded, boastful, servile, vague, competitive, anthropomorphic, or certain beyond the evidence.

Warmth means noticing the real concern without claiming private knowledge. Competence means specific work, current evidence, visible limits, and clear ownership.

## Platform-neutral and assumption-free rules

- Lead with the durable customer concern and relief, not a provider, channel, interface, category, or feature.
- Do not lead with listings, reviews, directories, dashboards, software, tools, or platform language.
- Name a mechanism later only when it helps the reader assess fit, proof, control, or compatibility.
- Never use a named provider as shorthand for the customer's whole discovery problem.
- Do not infer emotion, motive, skill, staffing, maturity, budget, current tools, or behavior from segment labels.
- Use customer facts only when the source, date, segment, and sampling limit are known.

## Language families

Eligible for proposal means structurally safe to explore. It does not mean approved wording.

| State | Language family | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligible for proposal | AI keeps changing | External condition only. Do not turn it into a race or customer deficit. |
| Eligible for proposal | You do not have to follow every change alone | Companionship without a product capability claim. Validate the exact phrasing with customers and by locale. |
| Eligible for proposal | Capable colleague, keeping watch, carrying supported work | Relationship posture only. Do not imply personhood, employment, omniscience, or unrestricted agency. |
| Eligible for proposal | Clear updates, retained control, evidence of what changed | Use as a value direction. Any product-specific version still needs proof. |
| Conditional | Monitors, prepares, acts, fixes, handles, delivers, works on your behalf | Requires current proof for object, cadence, channel, authority, control, receipt, verification, exceptions, and recovery. |
| Conditional | Not another dashboard, preferred-assistant access, automatic, continuous, always available | Requires route-specific product, security, reliability, accessibility, locale, and customer evidence. |
| Conditional | Numbers, coverage, speed, customer results, cases, scarcity, privacy, security | Requires dated source custody, exact scope, owner signoff, and release-time refresh. |
| Forbidden | Guaranteed discovery, recommendation, ranking, indexing, revenue, bookings, or external execution | These outcomes are not under Obenan's unilateral control. |
| Forbidden | Everything handled, every channel, never sleeps, set it and forget it, no oversight | Unlimited autonomy and universal coverage claims. |
| Forbidden | Stay ahead, fall behind, serious operators act now, competitors will win | Fear, shame, identity pressure, and unsupported urgency. |

## Compact claim-state ladder

Observed -> Inferred -> Recommended -> Prepared -> Approved -> Delivered -> Verified

Each state needs its own evidence. No rung proves the next. Delivered means the exact action reached the intended target with a receipt. Verified means an authoritative read-back or postcondition matched the stated success criteria. Neither state guarantees permanence or a business outcome.

Keep failed, partial, expired, superseded, revoked, rolled back, and unknown states visible.

## Fast tests

### Five-second test

After one brief view, can the intended reader state:

1. what human concern is being recognized;
2. what bounded relief Obenan offers;
3. why the claim is credible; and
4. what the next step is?

This is a qualitative StoryBrand diagnostic, not a scientific conversion threshold.

### Peer-voice test

Would a capable colleague say this beside the customer? The line should offer useful help without teaching from above, guessing at private feelings, performing intimacy, pressuring a decision, or hiding a limit.

## Illustrative microcopy patterns

The following patterns are noncanonical examples, not approved copy or product claims:

- Recognition: "AI keeps changing."
- Relief: "You do not have to follow every change alone."
- Control: "See what changed and what still needs your decision."
- Next step: "See what Obenan can handle for you."

## When uncertain

Use the narrowest truthful claim, label its evidence state, preserve the customer's control, and hold the wording for Product, claim, locale, legal, or founder review rather than filling the gap with confidence.

# Applied design guide

Use this file after the task router selects a rendered deliverable. The rules below apply the
public [brand system](BRAND_SYSTEM.md) and [design principles](DESIGN_PRINCIPLES_2026.md).

## Proposal

Recommended sequence:

1. Cover with the customer's desired outcome.
2. What we learned from them.
3. The verified gap or opportunity.
4. What Obenan will take over.
5. The plan and timing.
6. Investment, scope and decision path.

Keep the prospect's language. Do not add an audit-stage CTA after the commercial decision is
already being requested.

## Performance review

Use the mandatory [existing-client review standard](../../knowledge/engagement/EXISTING_CLIENT_REVIEW.md)
and [eleven-page template](../../templates/deck/EXISTING_CLIENT_REVIEW.html). They supersede the
generic sales-deck sequence for results reviews. Discovery first, customer photo cover,
per-location comparisons, practical switches and one bounded next step.

Do not hide a decline. Explain what is measured, what is inferred and what needs investigation.

## New pitch

Lead with one real finding about the prospect. If no finding is available, label the output as
a template. Use no customer-specific number until it is verified.

## Customer proof

Approved customer proof may be used only as recorded in
`../../knowledge/proof/PUBLIC_PROOF_REGISTER.md`. Keep the measurement window, geography and
approximate wording with the metrics. Do not copy internal account structure into a page.

## Useful page patterns

### Evidence page

- eyebrow: scope and period
- headline: the finding
- large number: the primary metric
- one comparison or trend
- source and capture date
- one sentence explaining why it matters

### Diagnosis page

- four short gaps at most
- each gap has signal, consequence and owner
- one key takeaway panel
- no red-wall alarm styling

### Plan page

- three to five steps
- each step has action, owner and timing
- distinguish customer action from Obenan action
- show dependencies plainly

### Investment page

- use authorized pricing only
- show scope beside price
- state billing period and tax treatment when supplied
- avoid artificial urgency or unapproved discounts
- finish with one decision CTA

## Charts

- Bars for categorical comparison.
- Lines for time series with a visible period.
- Tables for exact mappings.
- Timelines for dependent steps.
- A large number only when scope and source sit beside it.

Never use a pie, doughnut or gauge. Never crop an axis to exaggerate change.

## Final visual inspection

- No overflow, clipping or accidental blank page.
- No isolated heading at the bottom of a page.
- Source labels remain legible in print.
- Logo aspect ratio is intact.
- Only one accent color appears outside the canonical logo.
- All customer metrics match the evidence record.
- The final page contains one clear next step.

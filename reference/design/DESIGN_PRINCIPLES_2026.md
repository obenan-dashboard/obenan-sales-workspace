# Design principles for sales material

These principles turn Obenan evidence into clear proposals, reports and presentations. Read
[BRAND_SYSTEM.md](BRAND_SYSTEM.md) for the exact public document palette and typography.

## 1. Evidence is the visual hierarchy

The strongest verified fact should be the strongest object on the page. A headline, number,
comparison or quote earns prominence only when its source and scope are known.

## 2. One page, one job

A page should answer one question: what changed, what is missing, why it matters, what Obenan
does, what the investment is, or what happens next. Split pages that try to do several jobs.

## 3. Calm beats spectacle

Use whitespace, alignment and contrast. Avoid glass effects, floating decoration, oversized
icons, stock “AI” imagery, gradient fog, fake dashboards and ornamental charts.

## 4. One accent, used with purpose

Choose one approved accent for emphasis. Use it for the key bar, rule, step or CTA. Do not use
the palette as confetti, and do not encode magnitude by hue.

## 5. Show states, not theatre

When describing Obenan's work, use truthful states such as observed, verified, scheduled,
published, waiting or needs approval. Never fabricate live activity, progress percentages,
typing indicators or system output.

## 6. Make comparisons fair

- Show a common baseline.
- State the period and geography.
- Use consistent scales.
- Distinguish totals from rates.
- Do not imply causality from correlation.
- Put assumptions beside the result, not in a hidden footnote.

## 7. Make uncertainty visible

Use plain labels: measured, customer-provided, public source, estimate, inference or pending
verification. A clear limitation strengthens the work.

## 8. Design for the decision

Lead from the customer's situation to the decision they need to make. A strong deck usually
moves through: context, finding, consequence, plan, proof, investment and next step.

## 9. Respect customer identity

The prospect is the hero. Do not make Obenan visually dominate every page. Never use another
customer's name, logo, quote or metric outside the scope approved in
`../../knowledge/proof/PUBLIC_PROOF_REGISTER.md`.

## 10. Build for print and reuse

- A4 at 100% scale.
- No clipped text, orphaned headings or accidental fifth page.
- Minimum comfortable body size.
- High contrast and non-color status labels.
- Exact Obenan logo assets, never recreated.
- A companion evidence note for customer-specific figures.

## Preflight

Before handover, confirm:

1. Every customer figure has a source and capture date.
2. Every page has one clear job and one focal object.
3. The selected accent is the only decorative color.
4. No private identifiers, correspondence or account structure appear.
5. No unsupported claim, ranking promise or named-partner claim appears.
6. The exported PDF has the intended page count and no overflow.
7. The CTA names one concrete next step.

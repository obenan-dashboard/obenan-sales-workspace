## Use in sales material

- Use exact local files with meaningful alt text. Preserve proportions, colors and clear
  space. Do not redraw, stretch, crop, trace or generate substitutes.
- PDFs use a static logo group, without carousel animation, fading edges or overflow clipping.
  Inspect every mark after rendering. The prospect template supplies the approved group.
- Results reviews still lead with their own customer; do not add a competing logo wall to
  every customer report.
- Approval covers brand-name/logo proof. Separate testimonials, metrics, current contract
  status and transaction delivery need their own evidence. Do not infer a fresh location
  total from the nine marks or copy the website's 1,500-location line as an audited count.
- Withdrawals update this index, register, template and integrity tests together.
